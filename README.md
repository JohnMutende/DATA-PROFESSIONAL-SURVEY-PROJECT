# DATA-PROFESSIONAL-SURVEY-PROJECT
**Project Overview**
This project involves analyzing Data Professional Survey data using Microsoft Power BI to identify trends, patterns, and key performance indicators (KPIs). The analysis focuses on different dimensions such as job roles, salaries, programming languages, countries, career entry, age, and job satisfaction.

***
**Objectives**
+ **Survey Respondents Analysis**
Determine the total number of survey respondents and their average age.

+ **Salary Analysis by Job Title**
Compare average salaries across different data professional job titles to identify higher- and lower-paying roles.

+ **Programming Language Analysis**
Determine the most popular programming languages among data professionals and compare preferences across different job roles.

+ **Country Analysis**
Analyze the distribution of survey respondents by country to identify countries with the highest number of participants.

+ **Career Entry Analysis**
Determine how difficult respondents found it to enter the data profession.

+ **Job Satisfaction Analysis**
Evaluate respondents' satisfaction with salary and work/life balance using satisfaction ratings.

+ **Career and Job Preferences**
Identify the most important factors respondents consider when looking for a new job.

+ **Insights & KPIs**
Derive at least 5 actionable insights from the dashboard, including salary trends, programming-language preferences, career entry difficulty, geographical distribution, and job satisfaction.

***
**Data Source**
I extracted these data from a wide range of samples from Kaggle

***
**Tools Used**
+ Microsoft excel
+ Microsoft power BI

  ***
  **Recommendations**
 + Organizations should review salary structures for data professionals based on role and experience.
   
 +  Aspiring data professionals should focus on highly demanded programming and analytical skills.
   
 +  Training institutions should provide practical programs that make it easier for beginners to enter the data profession.
   
 +   Employers should improve salary and work/life balance where satisfaction levels are low.
   
 +    Organizations can use the findings to better understand the skills and expectations of data professionals when recruiting and retaining employees.
